Overall problem

Every order crosses eight teams, and no single system carries it from start to finish

  • Two ERPs, no link. BMS and LincolnERP are updated side by side.
  • Chat fills the gaps. Teams pass updates on WhatsApp, Teams or email.
  • Data moves by CSV. Exported from one system, filtered in Excel, imported into another.
  • Every hand-off is manual. Each one adds errors, rework and slower cash.

How to read this map

  • Blocks are departments. The number on each label is its step in the order journey: 1–4 follow the order from sales request to invoice, 5–8 are the teams that support it.
  • Lines are hand-offs between teams. A red ring marks where information breaks or has to be re-keyed. Click a line or ring for detail.
  • Overall problem opens the company-wide summary. The bar above the map lights up where each of the four shared problems appears.
  • Click a block to see its roles and headcount, every problem ranked by severity, and its broken hand-offs.
  • Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, press Esc to step back.
